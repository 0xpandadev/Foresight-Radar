## Promotion Rules

Promote to Core when:

- the source repeatedly produces high-signal primary or early evidence
- dates and URLs are stable
- noise is low enough to check every run

Keep in Watch when:

- the source is useful but periodic, promotional, or narrow

Reject when:

- the source duplicates others, lacks dates, is mostly SEO content, or repeatedly misleads
