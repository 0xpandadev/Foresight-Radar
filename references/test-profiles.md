# Test Profiles

These are validation examples, not a fixed list of supported themes.

## tech-risk-security

Type: horizontal

Scope:

- AI risk
- cybersecurity
- IT risk
- model risk
- third-party and vendor risk
- data privacy and breach events
- security regulation and governance
- enterprise risk management signals

Likely source types:

- cybersecurity agencies and regulators
- standards bodies
- incident databases
- security company research
- cloud and AI lab security blogs
- credible specialist media
- community signals only for early warnings

Noise to avoid:

- generic fear-based AI commentary
- vendor SEO posts with no new evidence
- unsourced breach rumors

## energy-utility-tech

Type: vertical / intersection

Scope:

- electric utilities
- grid and transmission
- generation and storage
- data-center power demand
- nuclear, renewables, and batteries when material
- utility software and grid tech
- energy regulation and market structure
- major company investments and partnerships

Likely source types:

- energy regulators and ministries
- utility IR and press releases
- grid operators
- energy agencies and data providers
- specialist power-market media
- technology vendors only when evidence is concrete

Noise to avoid:

- generic climate opinion
- commodity-price chatter unrelated to utilities or grid tech
- promotional vendor content without customer or deployment evidence
